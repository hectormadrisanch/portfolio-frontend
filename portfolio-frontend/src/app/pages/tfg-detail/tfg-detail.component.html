Web Platform for the Lifecycle Management and Tracking of Final Degree Projects

Final Degree Project encompassing end-to-end software engineering, from requirements gathering and C4 modeling to full-stack implementation.

01. The Context

Developed as the capstone project for my Computer Engineering degree, this platform was designed to solve complex management and tracking challenges. The primary focus was on establishing a rigorous software lifecycle, prioritizing scalable architecture and maintainable code over rapid prototyping.

02. Engineering & Architecture

The project relies on a robust architecture model, applying industry-standard design patterns to ensure separation of concerns and high cohesion.

  • System Design: Modeled using the C4 framework to map context, containers, and components clearly.
  • Database: Relational schema designed for strict data integrity and normalized to the third normal form (3NF).
  • Implementation: Full-stack development following SOLID principles and clean architecture guidelines.
